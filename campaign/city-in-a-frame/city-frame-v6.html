01Brand ki samajh

A premium township that should sell restraint, not noise.

The project is a premium township in East Pune, and its buyers are families who have already seen the brochures and the show flats of several similar projects.

The brand should therefore speak like a luxury hotel rather than a property ad, with two colours, one serif, one pattern and a calm voice that never shouts about discounts.

This campaign suits that voice, because a gold frame is a quiet and beautiful object that lets the life do the selling.

02Observations

What we see in every premium sales journey.

  • A buyer compares about 5 to 7 similar projects (our estimate), and every one of them shows the same specifications and the same amenities.
  • Families say that they understand the project but cannot feel it, and then they decide to visit once more next weekend.
  • The most relaxed moment of a family's week is a Sunday at the mall, when nobody is trying to sell them anything.

These are strategist observations to be validated with the sales team's visit data, and they are not research results.

03Insight

From observation to insight.

Families who are choosing a premium home feel unsure, because they understand the specifications but cannot picture the life. So the township can stop explaining the flat and let them step into the picture of a Tuesday morning there.

05 The idea as a six-piece brief

Six sentences.
One idea.

Business problem

A premium township in East Pune competes with five to seven similar projects, and its leads revisit again and again before deciding.

Consumer problem

Families know the specifications of the home but cannot feel the life, so they keep delaying the decision.

Insight

The real problem is not information but imagination.

Solution

A gold frame in the mall lets a family step in and receive one photo of the life they could have.

Consumer goal

The family wants to feel certain before the second visit.

Business goal

The township wants fewer revisits and a faster yes or no.

06Depth

The strategy under the idea, in plain agency terms.

ICPFamilies in Pune with a household income that supports a premium purchase, on a shortlist of several projects, visiting malls on weekends.
Not forWalk-in bargain hunters, because the idea sells a feeling and not a price.
Hook"Step into your Tuesday morning."
Reach planGold frames at premium malls and weekend spots near East Pune, supported by a small number of local residential and lifestyle creators.
Earned mediaThe framed photo is made to be shared, and every share places the township in a family's own social feed.
RetentionA visit invite follows within 48 hours, and the same family can return to choose another frame.
Go to marketStart with one mall for a month, read the frame-to-visit rate, and then add locations.
Restraint ruleNo persona photography and no discount language anywhere on the frame or the photo.
One call to actionStep into the frame.
Goal and timelineA 6-week pilot in one mall, with targets for frames, visits and days to decision set against the project's own baseline.

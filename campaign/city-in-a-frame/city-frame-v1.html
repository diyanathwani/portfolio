The idea

Don't tell people about the flat.
Put them in the picture.

You step in. You get a photo of your future life.

The problem

Premium homes in East Pune.
Everyone looks the same.

Buyers compare 5 to 7 projects.
They get all the information they need.
They still don't feel anything.

Goal: fewer revisits and faster yes/no from serious buyers.

The insight

The problem isn't information.
It's imagination.

People buy when they can see themselves there.
Buyers pick routines, not rooms.
So we sell the Tuesday morning, not the 2 BHK.

Launch in 3 phases

Phase 1
Experience

Frames at premium malls and weekend hotspots near East Pune.

Phase 2
Visit

Frame photos bring families to the township for a relaxed visit. Follow-up within 48 hours.

Phase 3
Decide

Fewer revisits, faster yes or no. The idea repeats for new launches and new cities.

Goal and how we would check it

Better leads, not louder ads.

Ads bring curious leads. Experience brings leads with intent. Targets are set against the project's own baseline.

  • Frames taken per weekend
  • Frame-to-visit rate
  • Revisits per buyer
  • Days from first visit to decision

Before

Buyers get a brochure, a site visit and a discount. They go home unsure.

After

Buyers already saw themselves living there. The visit feels like a yes.

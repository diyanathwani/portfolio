The brief, on one page.

1 Business problem

A housing project in Nagpur has to turn visits into bookings. Break-even needs about 5 homes (projected).

2 Consumer problem

Buying a home feels like leaving one. The old house holds the memories. The new one is bare walls.

3 Insight

People don't move homes. Stories do.

4 Solution

Recreate one old family photo in the new home. Share it. Walls That Raised Us.

5 Consumer goal

Know it can feel like home, before moving in.

6 Business goal

More family visits, fewer revisits, faster yes. 2-3x site-office visits (projected).

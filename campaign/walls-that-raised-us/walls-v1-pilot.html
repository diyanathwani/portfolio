The idea

TAKE AN OLD PHOTO.
PUT IT IN THE NEW HOME.

Families copy one childhood photo, pose for pose, inside their new flat.
Same faces. New walls.

The problem

UPGRADING A HOME IS EASY.
UPDATING EMOTIONS
IS NOT.

Nagpur families want a better home. But moms don't want to leave behind the pencil lines on the wall that show how tall the kids were.

It was never about price. It was about leaving the memories behind.

The goal: get families to visit and decide.
Projected target: 5 homes sold pays back the campaign.

The insight

PEOPLE DON'T MOVE HOMES.
STORIES DO.

Parents carry memories

The festival mornings. The exam nights. The hallway cricket.

Kids carry dreams

They want a better room, better space, a better future.

The deal closes when both feel respected.

Diwali week9 PMeveryone is home
The moment

THE NIGHT THE WHOLE FAMILY IS UNDER ONE ROOF.

Big home decisions don't happen on a random Tuesday. They happen when the kids are back from college, the grandparents are visiting, and the diyas are lit.

That is when we show up. Emotion opens the door. Timing closes the deal.

The one thing people do

RECREATE
ONE MEMORY.

A stranger can join in under a minute. All they need is an old family photo and a phone.

#WallsThatRaisedUsShare the memory

  1. Find the photo

    Dig out that old print from the album. The one with the height marks in the back.

  2. Scan it

    Point your phone at it, or upload a picture. No app to download.

  3. See it again

    The filter shows the same moment, rebuilt inside the new home.

  4. Share it

    Post it with #WallsThatRaisedUs. Friends tag their own old photos.

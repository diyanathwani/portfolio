Spec concept for Zomato

Read.
The Note.

Every order has a story in the note box.

Zomato turns the line you type for your rider into the most human ad in India. You write it. A real person writes back.

Add a note for the delivery partner
Bhaiya, bell mat bajana. Baby abhi soyi hai.

The idea

The note box is where
India gets most honest.

Nobody sees these notes but one rider.
We make the rider reply, on the bag, in one line.
And the best notes of the week become real things.

The problem

Zomato is loved.
Ordering is still a habit.

Most brands in food delivery look the same at 10 PM: a photo, a discount, a button.
Zomato already wins on wit. It has no ritual yet that a customer does in the app and shows a friend.

Goal: make the note box a thing people open on purpose.
Gate pe kutta hai. Darna mat, woh sirf pyaar karta hai.
Nani ko thoda zor se bolna, sunai kam deta hai.
Exam kal hai. Please jaldi. Mummy ne kaha maggi banane ka time nahi.
Thanda mat lana. Lekin gaadi dheere chalana.
The insight

People write to a stranger
more kindly than to us.

The note box is tiny. It has no ad, no like button, no audience.
That is why the lines in it are real.
We are not inventing a moment. We are noticing one.

10:12PM, hostel, third floor
The audience moment

Phone in one hand.
Thumb over the note box.

Who: 18 to 30, ordering for one or two, mostly from a metro or a tier-2 city.
The moment: the second before they tap "Place order", when they decide whether to type something.

The mechanic

Note Back.
One line in. One line out.

1

Type

Write any line in the note box. Tick "reply to me" if you want a response.
2

Rider replies

Rider taps one of 6 preset reply cards, written by the Zomato team. No typing on a bike.
3

Bag sticker

Your note and the reply print on a sticker on the bag. You peel it. You post it.
4

Note of the Week

Opt-in notes are shared without names. Top 5 get a real reply from a restaurant.

The restaurant menu

Mockups made by the author. Notes shown are written for this concept, not taken from real customers.

Launch in 3 phases

Week 1-2
Plant it

Reply cards live for riders in 2 pilot cities. Stickers on a small share of orders. No ads.

Week 3-6
Share it

First Note of the Week goes out. Creators post their own sticker. Restaurants write replies.

Week 7-10
Grow it

Open to all cities. Best notes become a limited menu line and a short film cut from real replies.

Goal and how we would check it

One number. Four checks.

Target: 1 in 10 orders in pilot cities carries a note by week 8. This is a target, to be set against Zomato's own baseline.

  • Share of orders with a note, before vs after
  • Share of notes that ask for a reply
  • Stickers posted with the campaign tag
  • Repeat orders from people who got a reply

Before

The note box is a utility. Ordering is quiet and forgettable.

After

The note box is a ritual. People write for the reply and show it off.

Zomato already has the voice.
This gives it a ritual.

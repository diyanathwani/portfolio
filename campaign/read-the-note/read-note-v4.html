Business problem

Food delivery apps look the same at 10 PM. Loyalty is thin.

Consumer problem

Ordering is a transaction. Nobody feels seen.

Insight

People write kinder lines to a stranger than to anyone else.

Solution

Note Back. The rider replies, the bag becomes the post.

Business goal

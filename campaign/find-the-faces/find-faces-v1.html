The idea

Make talent hunting
public and competitive.

A poster at the chai stall.
A 15-second video.
A leaderboard where your college fights for first place.

The problem

Talent is everywhere.
Opportunity is not.

Campuses make creators every year.
Their fame ends at the campus gate.
Brands pay for reach. Beginners have no way in.

Goal: a first stop for new creators, before agencies and brands find them.
"He is famous in college... but nowhere else."
Hostel singer. 40 followers. 1 great voice.
Algorithm says: post more. Nobody says: who sees it.
The insight

College builds creators.
Not careers.

Students are driven by identity, visibility and belonging.
Make a win visible to the whole campus, and they start to care.
Hiding it in an algorithm does the opposite.

4:30PM, canteen, chai in hand
The audience moment

Waiting for chai.
Eyes on the wall poster.

Who: college students, 18 to 24, in any city.
The moment: five idle minutes at the canteen, library or hostel notice board, phone already in hand.

The mechanic

Scan. Drop. Rank. Share.

1

Scan

Scan the QR on a poster at your chai stall, library or canteen.
2

Drop

Record 15 seconds. Sing, dance, joke, anything. No editing needed.
3

Rank

Every entry adds points to your college. The leaderboard moves live.
4

Share

Friends share the clip. "She is from my batch!" Friends beat brands.

Launch in 3 phases

Phase 1
Pilot

50 campuses. Posters at canteens, libraries and chai stalls. Founding faces seeded as first entries.

Phase 2
Rivalry

Leaderboards go live. Meme pages and campus pages push "beat your rival college".

Phase 3
Platform

Year 1: grow into the first discovery step for new creators, with brand matches on top.

Goal and how we would check it

One pilot. Four checks.

Pilot plan (projected): 150K reach and 10K scans across 50 campuses. These are planning targets, not results.

  • Scans to videos posted
  • Videos per college, and repeat posting
  • Clips shared outside the app
  • Creators who get a brand enquiry

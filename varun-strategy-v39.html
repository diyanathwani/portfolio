THE REST OF THE SYSTEM

New Highlights

A first-visit path through Start here, Camera years, Founder work, Books to film, Stage voice and In the news. Each uses real proof, a clear purpose and a next step.

Six Reel series

The camera before the company | One decision, one cost | The room after the talk | Before the feed | Who is this character? | Student questions. Question first, real footage and verified firsthand answers next.

The existing broadcast channel

A four-week return plan for The Enthu Cutlets: reintroduce, share one real decision, give a casual note, then close the loop. Not a proposal to launch a channel that already exists.

Measurement, not a promise

Start with a 14-day first-party Insights baseline. Track first-seconds hold, watch time, saves and shares per reach, profile visits to follows, link taps and next-message return.

What I made

Profile diagnosis | bio/search options | pinned-post hierarchy | new Highlight concepts | 21 carousel slides | written captions and CTAs | Reel series | broadcast examples | measurement plan.

What this does not claim

No profile edits or content were published. Public views do not prove retention or causality. Growth targets need account Insights. Stories, personal positions and rights need review before use.

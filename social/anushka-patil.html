The audience / Target audience + ICP

Who the strategy is for.

1. Primary audience

Women in India, with Maharashtra as the clear focus. This replaced an earlier, loosely international target with no clear audience.

2. Ideal coaching client (ICP)

A woman who wants strength and an aesthetic physique without extreme diets - the 1:1 transformation offer. Starting from beginner language, not advanced gym-goer terms.

3. What she searches and wants

Hourglass, glutes, hip dips, broad shoulders but lean and feminine. The audience comes with a body goal or a beginner question, so each idea starts there. The intent shows in the numbers: about 24.6K saves and 45K shares in the 90 days to 5 October 2026.

4. Language + region

Hinglish hooks, Marathi songs and audio, a Maharashtra-first mix. 2 or 3 of her top ten reels use Marathi audio.

5. Why they stay: her story

A comeback story - international-level shooting, AVN, strength training, coaching - gives women a person to follow, not only advice. PCOD sits in her positioning.

6. How they find her

Mostly through discovery: 7,182,424 views in the 90 days to 5 October 2026, 57.4% of them from non-followers, and 238,409 profile visits. Then profile, DM TRANSFORM, a 1:1 conversation.

The strategist work / Pillars, formats, hooks

The content system behind the posts.

Content pillars

1. Story + vulnerability

Her own journey leads: setbacks, rebuilding, the second innings. The person carries the advice, not the other way round.

e.g. Motivation + personal story reel - 1.5M views, reported.
2. Opinions + hot takes

Her real opinions, in her voice. Pointer-based scripts make a hot take easy to deliver on camera.

e.g. Genetics / satire hook - 4.2M views; sponsored content inside an engaging hook.
3. Beginner how-to

Words women actually search - hourglass, glutes, hip dips, broad shoulders but lean and feminine - instead of advanced gym terminology. The keyword enters the cover, opening line and caption.

e.g. Hourglass how-to - 597K views.
4. Regional identity

India-first, Maharashtra at the centre, Marathi audio in the mix - replacing an unfocused international target.

e.g. Regional song + gym reel - 11.2M views, reported. 2-3 of the top ten reels run Marathi audio.
5. Fun + trends

Trending audio, dance and curiosity open loops keep the account light between story and opinion posts.

e.g. Curiosity + dance - 7.2M, "Manjulika 2.0" riff - 4.7M, outfit question - 9.4M (all reported).
